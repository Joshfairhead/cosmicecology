### Pillar Project: Drew, Franky, and the Dynamics of Colouring

When I was at Pillar there were two Americans brought in called Drew and Franky who had worked together in LA. Franky was a designer/programmer and Drew was the product owner. Franky was largely a workhorse that didn't interact so much with others, while Drew had regular product meetings with the Devs. The Devs were slow building the product to the point that Franky decided to do the whole thing solo while he was waiting around. From a CE perspective it would seem to me that Drew was the initiator, the devs were colouring and Franky was the outcome. He actually got pretty lucky as Drew would have regular meetings with the bossman, who he was also coloured by. I specifically remember Franky having a bitch to about no longer being himself as he didn't want to wear the fancy shirts he was wearing etc. which were in the style of the CEO, as he was 'hood'. Actually more than likely he was confused.
